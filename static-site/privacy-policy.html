Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information damirbuilds collects and how it's used. It's written to be clear rather than exhaustive, and it isn't a substitute for legal advice — if you need a policy tailored to specific compliance requirements (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) for your own use of this site, please consult a qualified professional.

Who this covers

damirbuilds (damirbuilds.com) is an independent, personally-run service built and operated by Damir. This policy applies to this website and to the "Get Started" onboarding flow used to purchase and set up an automation.

Information collected

The following is collected only when you choose to provide it — for example, by filling out the onboarding form or contacting me directly:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, and X (Twitter) handle, submitted through the "Get Started" form or by email/DM.
  • Setup preferences: the tools and posting frequency you select while configuring your automation (e.g. Airtable vs. Google Sheets, Make.com vs. n8n, posting cadence).
  • Payment confirmation details: whether you've completed payment, and an optional screenshot you choose to upload to confirm it. Actual payment processing (card, crypto, or bank details) happens entirely on the payment provider's own site — see "Payment processors" below. I never see or store your card, wallet, or bank credentials.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs collected automatically by the hosting provider (Cloudflare), such as IP address and browser type, used only for security and performance — not for tracking individuals.

The site does not currently run any analytics or advertising tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc.). If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

How your information is used

  • To set up and deliver the automation you're paying for.
  • To contact you about your order — confirming payment, coordinating setup, or answering questions.
  • To keep a record of who's paid and who's still owed a setup, for my own bookkeeping.

Your information is never sold, rented, or used for advertising to third parties.

Third-party services

A few outside services help run this site and process orders. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes the "Get Started" form submissions and routes them to my email.
  • Wise & NOWPayments — payment processors. When you choose to pay, you're taken to their site to complete the transaction; they handle and store your payment details directly, not damirbuilds.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and provides basic security/performance infrastructure.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site, which may involve a request to Google's servers.

Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) for two small, functional purposes:

  • Remembering your light/dark mode preference.
  • Autosaving your progress on the "Get Started" form so you don't lose your answers if you refresh the page. This draft is stored only in your own browser and is cleared automatically once your order is submitted.

Neither is used to track you across other websites.

Data retention

Order and contact information is kept for as long as needed to deliver your automation and for reasonable bookkeeping afterward. You can request deletion of your information at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

You can ask, at any time, to see what information is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Just email contact@damirbuilds.com and I'll take care of it directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a new tool or tracking script is added to the site — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can go to contact@damirbuilds.com or @damirbuilds on X.
