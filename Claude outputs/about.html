Why I specialize in migrations

Most Webflow developers build from scratch. Migrations are a different job: you're not just designing a site, you're protecting years of rankings, content, and traffic while rebuilding everything underneath it. That's the part most agencies rush, and it's the part I don't.

I've moved stores and sites off Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and plain HTML, plus built new sites in Webflow directly from Figma files. Every project starts with the same question: what can't afford to break? Then the audit, redirect map, and rebuild happen around that answer.

How I work

  • Your current site stays live and untouched until the new one is tested and approved.
  • Every URL that currently ranks gets a mapped redirect, no exceptions.
  • If Webflow isn't the right move for your site, I'll tell you that on the free call instead of taking the project anyway.
