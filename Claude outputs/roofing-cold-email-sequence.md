# Roofing Cold Email Sequence

Built to work with the landing page at `/roofing-speed-to-lead.html` (link: `https://damirbuilds.com/roofing-speed-to-lead`). All three emails point to the same page and the same offer, a free 15-minute speed-to-lead audit call, so the story is consistent from inbox to page.

**Format notes for whatever tool you send these from (Instantly, Smartlead, Apollo, etc.):**
- Plain text, no images, no HTML template. Cold email performs worse the more it looks like marketing.
- `{{FirstName}}` and `{{CompanyName}}` are placeholders, swap for your tool's actual merge tags.
- Send from a real person's address (yours), not a no-reply.
- Every email needs a working reply-to and, per CAN-SPAM, a way to opt out, most cold email tools handle unsubscribe automatically; if yours doesn't, add a one-line "reply STOP to opt out" to email 1.
- Suggested spacing: Email 1 on day 1, Email 2 on day 4, Email 3 on day 9. Adjust to your tool's deliverability guidance.
