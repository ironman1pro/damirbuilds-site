Here's what's actually costing you jobs

The first roofer to respond usually wins the job. Storm-damage leads go out to multiple companies at once. If your crew is on a roof and the office can't pick up, that lead is already talking to someone else.

Storm season turns your phone into a bottleneck. A single system that handles ten calls a day can't suddenly handle sixty, so most of them go to voicemail or an inbox nobody checks until tomorrow.

Leads that don't hear back in a few hours go cold. By the time someone gets around to calling back the next day, the homeowner already booked an inspection with whoever called first.
